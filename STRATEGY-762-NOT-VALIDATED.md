## Denominator and exact cost contract

`V` is all included holdings' EUR market values plus signed included cash from the canonical allocation universe. Every security exposure must map completely to the four chosen existing strategy classes; unsupported/manual, unmapped, incompatible, missing, stale or estimated holdings suppress purchase amounts instead of disappearing. Known cash is kept outside the four security categories and displayed separately; missing cash FX yields null, not zero. Cash-only unknown class labels do not make otherwise known cash vanish.

New external rate `R` increases total portfolio value once. Default funding is `R`; explicit cash opt-in adds `max(0, cash)` to the funding. Since that cash already belongs to `V`, deficits always use target × `(V + R)`, avoiding double-counting it when moved into holdings. No wealth threshold changes the strategy weights. Each category budget is apportioned among its eligible buy products using their internal underweights; held non-buy products stay in the category denominator. Stable ties follow the saved route order.

Category/product budget includes configured transaction fees. The solver chooses the largest affordable gross amount or whole unit-step quantity with gross + fixed fee + upward-rounded percentage fee within that budget. A minimum is a gross minimum before fees. If it cannot be met, no purchase or fee is charged in the projection. No unspent amount is silently redistributed; gross + fees + residual always equals funding. Null constraints are unknown, not zero fees. Existing valuation rules price quantities; no broker order or guaranteed execution price/date is implied.
