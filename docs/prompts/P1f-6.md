Owner decision (30.09.2026): spending on a credit card follows **YNAB's rule**, not the simpler concept rule. Today `budgetMonths` moves the full amount of every categorised card spend into the card's `card_payment` envelope and treats any overspending as "ungedeckt" (reduces next month's Zu verteilen). YNAB instead distinguishes:

- **Funded card spending**: the part of a card spend that the category could cover moves from the category to the card payment envelope.
- **Credit overspending**: the part the category could not cover is *not* moved; it stays as new debt on the card (the card balance is larger than its payment envelope). At month end the category resets to 0 like any overspending, but credit overspending does **not** reduce next month's Zu verteilen (it was never cash), while **cash overspending** does.
- Covering an overspent category later in the same month (assigning or moving money into it) retroactively funds the card part: the payment envelope then receives that amount.
- Refunds on the card in a category move money back from the payment envelope into the category (full amount); payments to the card from a budget account reduce the payment envelope.
