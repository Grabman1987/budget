## Manual same-statement difference reconciliation — NOT VALIDATED

This later bounded source checkpoint extends the existing optional `statementBonusEvidenceSchema` JSON, with no migration or new financial writer. The owner may retain the existing explicit-bonus path or select manual line reconciliation. In the latter path, every selected original purchase split has exactly one distinct page/line locator and entered charged cents from the retained PDF of the same accepted statement. The strict schema binds those lines one-to-one to the selected split identities, requires nonnegative charged cents no greater than the unchanged gross split, and checks the exact positive gross-minus-charged total and per-category allocations. Fully matched zero-difference splits are allowed but add no credit; all-zero, negative, unmatched, duplicate, invalid or incomplete evidence cannot confirm a reconciled bonus. The owner expressly confirms completeness and absence of partial or previous credit, and reviews existing credits and fee/adjustment context. The accepted evidence remains bound to source revision/hash/account, original booking/split CAS, idempotency and the existing audit/Undo group. Represented-source reassessment compares the new line evidence as well as financial terms so it cannot silently replace or omit the association. The card purchase, issued statement payable, transfer and separate credit writer are unchanged.

The interface does not extract or authenticate PDF transaction rows independently; correctness of the manually entered match requires owner review of the retained private statement. Partial or previously booked credit, uncertain fee/adjustment mixtures, ambiguous or missing rows, and zero-only net purchases remain unresolved/manual and cannot use the new calculation. No tests, test edits, checks, typechecks, builds, browser/Axe, CI, PR, merge or deployment were performed for this checkpoint. Independent source review and later runtime, accessibility and private-source acceptance are still required; this is not LIVE.
